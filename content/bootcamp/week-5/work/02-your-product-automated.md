By the end of this week the product you built in week three answers its own questions and does some of its own work.

## What to build

1. **A chatbot on your week three site.** Built into your site as lesson five shows (or, if you have a clear reason, a no-code builder embedded on it). Clearly labelled as an AI assistant, answering from a knowledge file about your product.
2. **One automation doing real work for the product.** In n8n, Zapier or Make. For example: new sign-up, so a welcome email and a row in a sheet; new order, so a confirmation and a morning delivery plan.
3. **Your AI operating system folder,** set up as lesson six shows, with hard rules including "nothing goes out without my approval".

## What to hand in

The live link to your site. In the note:

- your chatbot's rules (the part of its instructions after the knowledge)
- what your automation does, with a screenshot of its execution history
- the spend limit you set on your API account

## What approval looks like

- The chatbot is live on the site, says it is an AI assistant, and answers a question about your product correctly.
- Asked something that is not in its knowledge, it says it is not sure and points to a real way to reach you. It does not invent an answer.
- It does not ask visitors for card details, ID numbers or photos.
- The automation has run successfully at least three times.
- No API key or secret appears anywhere in the site, the repository or the screenshots.
- A spend limit is set.

It does not need to be clever. It needs to be honest, safe and working. Week six turns everything you have built into a job, clients or a business.
