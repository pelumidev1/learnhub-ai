Build one automation in n8n that runs on a schedule and does something useful for your business. It can be small. It has to be real, and it has to have run on its own.

1. Pick a job you do by hand at the same time every day or week: a morning order summary, a weekly sales total, a reminder to follow up with enquiries.
2. Build it in n8n with a **schedule trigger**, at least one step that reads real data, and one step that sends the result to you.
3. Adding an AI step is encouraged, not required.
4. Let it run on schedule at least twice, on your laptop left on, or on the n8n Cloud trial.

Hand in a link to a folder (Google Drive or similar, anyone with the link can view) containing:

- a screenshot of the whole workflow on the canvas
- a screenshot of **Executions** showing at least two scheduled runs
- a screenshot of what it sent you

In the note, say what job it replaces and roughly how much time that saves you each week. No credentials or API keys in any screenshot.
