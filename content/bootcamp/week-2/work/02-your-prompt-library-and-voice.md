By the end of this week you have prompts you can reuse, AI writing that sounds like you, and the words for the thing you build next week.

## What to build

1. **Your prompt library.** At least six prompts you have used for real this week and would use again, each with context, a role and constraints. Cover at least three kinds of work: posts, replies, proposals, captions, research. For each one, add a line on when to use it.
2. **Your voice skill, improved.** The skill from the assignment, with whatever the test showed was missing now fixed.
3. **One piece of writing, published in your voice.** A LinkedIn post, an article, a newsletter or a long Instagram caption, written with your voice skill and posted publicly under your name.
4. **Your launch copy for week three.** For the thing you will build next week: your positioning in one sentence, your one-line offer, and landing page copy with a headline, three reasons and a button label. Nothing on it you cannot prove.

## What to hand in

One Google Doc or Notion page, set so anyone with the link can view it, with the prompt library, the voice skill and the launch copy in it. Put the link to your published piece at the top.

## What approval looks like

- The published piece is live, public and reads like a person wrote it: none of the tells from lesson four.
- Every prompt in the library is one you actually used, not a copy of the lesson's examples.
- The launch copy names a real audience and a real offer, with a price or a clear next step.
- No [CHECK] marks are left in the copy.

The launch copy matters most. Week three puts it on a live site, and week four sells the same offer in a video.
