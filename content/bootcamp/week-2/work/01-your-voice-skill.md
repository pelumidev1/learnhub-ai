One skill and one test.

**1. Your voice skill.** The `SKILL.md` you built in lesson four, from five to ten pieces of your own writing. It needs a Never list and at least two real examples of your writing in "What good looks like".

**2. The test.** Pick one paragraph you need anyway: a post, a bio, a reply to a customer. Write it twice, from the same notes:

- once in a fresh chat with no skill
- once with your voice skill

Put both in a Google Doc, the one without the skill first, and label them. Under them, write two or three lines on what changed.

Hand in a link to the Doc, set so anyone with the link can view it, with the skill file pasted at the bottom.

If the two versions read almost the same, hand it in anyway and say so in the note. It means the skill needs more Never lines or a better example, and that is worth finding out this week, before your project depends on it.
