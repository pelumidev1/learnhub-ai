One shot, five to eight seconds, made with AI video, that someone scrolling past would believe was filmed.

1. Take a real photo of your real product, or of the thing your week three site is about, in daylight, on your phone.
2. Write the shot in the seven parts from lesson two: subject, action, setting, camera, light, style, sound.
3. Generate it in Google Flow on your free daily credits. Change one thing between attempts.
4. Check it for the giveaways: hands, text in the scene, too-perfect light, impossible physics.

Hand in a link to the clip (Google Drive or similar, set so anyone with the link can view it). In the note:

- the photo you started from
- your final prompt
- how many attempts it took, and the one change that made the biggest difference

A shot that took ten attempts and a clear note on what you learned counts for more than a lucky first try.
