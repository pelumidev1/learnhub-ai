Long conversations go bad. The model is carrying every wrong turn you took
together, and it keeps returning to them.

The signs are consistent. It repeats a mistake you already corrected. Answers
get shorter and vaguer. You are spending more effort steering than you would
spend starting again.

When that happens, open a new chat. Take the best version of what you have,
paste it in as the starting point, and say what you want changed. You lose the
history, which is the whole point, and you keep the work.

This feels wasteful and it is not. A fresh conversation with good context beats
a long one carrying baggage.

## What you should be able to do after this

Notice a stuck conversation within a couple of exchanges instead of twenty, and
restart without losing your work.
