Everything in this bootcamp ends with something shipped. This week it is you.

## What you are making

Three things, rebuilt with AI and posted publicly where people can see them:

1. **A bio.** Short. What you do, who for.
2. **A CV.** Current, honest, readable on a phone.
3. **A one-line offer.** What someone can hire you to do, in one sentence.

## Why this first

Because you will use all three for the next five weeks. Week six is about
selling what you built, and you cannot sell from a standing start.

And because it is the fastest way to find out whether you can actually direct
these tools. If you can get a model to describe you accurately, you can get it
to do most things.

## Posting it publicly is part of the deliverable

Not for the audience. For the commitment. Something posted is finished in a way
something in a drafts folder never is.

## What you should be able to do after this

Point at three things on the internet that describe you, all made this week,
none of which you wrote from a blank page.
