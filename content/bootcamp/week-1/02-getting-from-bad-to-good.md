The gap between people who get a lot out of AI and people who do not is almost
never the first prompt. It is what they do with the first answer.

## Say what is wrong, specifically

"Make it better" gets you a different flavour of the same thing. "The second
paragraph reads like a brochure, and I never said I was award winning" gets you
a fix.

## Give it an example of what right looks like

If you have a piece of writing you like, paste it and say "match this voice".
Showing beats describing, every time.

## Ask it to critique its own output

"What is weakest about this?" often surfaces the thing you were about to say
anyway, and it fixes it in the same turn.

## Know when to stop

Three rounds of edits and no progress means the conversation is stuck. That is
the next lesson.

## What you should be able to do after this

Take an answer you were not happy with and get it to something usable in three
exchanges or fewer.
