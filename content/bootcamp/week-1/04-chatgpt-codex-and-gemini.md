Claude is your home base. This lesson sets up the other two so they are ready when you need a second opinion, an image, or something from your Google files.

## You do not need to pay for these

ChatGPT and Gemini both have free plans that cover everything this bootcamp asks of them. Both also sell cheaper plans billed in local currency in some countries. If you want one later, fine, but you do not need it for the course.

## ChatGPT: Projects and memory

ChatGPT has Projects that work much like Claude's: a space with its own instructions and files. It also has memory, which you can review in its settings.

Do the same as you did in Claude. Check what it remembers about you. Create one project for the bootcamp and give it the same few lines about you. Now either assistant can pick up your work.

## What happened to custom GPTs

You will see a lot of tutorials about building custom GPTs. Skip them.

OpenAI stopped letting personal accounts create new GPTs, on every plan including the paid ones, and existing GPTs are scheduled to stop working on 11 December 2026. They are being replaced by plugins.

The interesting part is what happens to a GPT when it moves over: its instructions become a **skill**. That is the same idea you will build yourself in lessons eight and nine. You are learning the version that is replacing GPTs, not the one being retired.

## Codex: a second pair of eyes on code

Codex is OpenAI's coding agent. It is included on the free plan, with limited use.

You will build with Claude Code on this course, not Codex. But from week three, when Claude Code has written something you do not fully understand, Codex is a good second reader. Ask it to review the code and explain what could break. Same rule as before: never let one AI mark its own homework.

## Gemini: build a Gem

A Gem is Gemini's version of a saved assistant: a name, instructions and optional files, free on a personal Google account.

On your laptop, go to gemini.google.com, open the sidebar, choose **Gems**, then **New Gem**. Make one called **Second opinion**. For its instructions, write: "You review work someone else made. Say plainly what is weak, unclear or wrong, most important first. Do not rewrite it unless I ask."

Now every time you want an honest critique, you have a place to paste it.

## When to use which

| You want to | Open |
|---|---|
| Write, think something through, or build | Claude |
| An honest critique of Claude's work | Your Gemini Gem, or a fresh ChatGPT chat |
| An image | ChatGPT |
| Something from your Gmail, Docs or Drive | Gemini |
| A review of code Claude Code wrote | Codex |

## What you should be able to do after this

Reach for ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini on purpose, for the job each does best, with your context already set up in each.
