Articles about AI and jobs are full of confident numbers that contradict each other. This lesson skips them. You will learn where the work is moving, and then how to check the demand yourself, in your city, this week, which is worth more than any global statistic.

## Where the work is moving

The pattern is the same in every industry: **AI does the first draft, people direct it and check it.** Work that used to take a team now takes one person who knows how to direct AI and keep the quality high. That person is who you have spent six weeks becoming.

## Six industries

| Industry | What AI is changing | Who they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing and media** | Content, ads and video made in hours instead of weeks | People who can direct AI and keep the quality high |
| **Customer service** | AI agents answer the first line of questions | People who can build and manage those agents |
| **Operations and admin** | Data entry, scheduling and reporting are being automated | People who can design those workflows |
| **Finance and accounting** | Bookkeeping and routine checks are going automatic | People who can set it up and check the output |
| **Legal and professional services** | Research and first drafts take a fraction of the time | People who know where AI helps and where it fails |
| **Small businesses** | Owners want websites, content and automation without a big team | Freelancers who can deliver all three |

Match your path from lesson one to one or two of these. Chidi, heading for automation, looks at operations and finance. Amaka looks at small businesses: home bakers like her.

## Check the demand yourself

Spend one hour on this. It will tell you more than any report.

1. **Search job adverts.** On LinkedIn Jobs, and on the job boards people use in your country, search for the work, not the title: "automation", "n8n", "Zapier", "AI content", "chatbot", "Claude", "prompt". Filter to your city and to remote.
2. **Read ten adverts properly.** Copy them into your Claude Project and ask: *"Across these ten adverts, which skills and tools come up most? Which of them have I already used in the projects in my context folder? What is missing?"*
3. **Note the words they use.** Those exact words go on your CV and LinkedIn page next lesson.

For the business route, search the other way round: look at local businesses in your chosen industry online. Which have no website, a broken one, slow replies on WhatsApp, or the same post every week? Each of those is a problem you can now solve.

## Talk to five businesses

Adverts show what companies say they want. Conversations show what they will actually pay for.

Message five people who run or work in a business in your chosen industry. Not to sell. To ask:

> *"I am learning to use AI to save businesses time. What takes up hours in your week that you wish you did not have to do?"*

Write down their answers word for word. Amaka asked five home bakers. Four said the same thing: answering the same price and delivery questions on WhatsApp, all day. That is her first offer, and it is the chatbot she already built for herself.

## What you should be able to do after this

Say which industries are changing fastest for your path, find real adverts and real problems in your own market, and pull the exact words employers and customers use so your CV and your offer speak their language.
