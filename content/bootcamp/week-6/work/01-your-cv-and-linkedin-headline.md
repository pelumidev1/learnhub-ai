Rebuild your CV and your LinkedIn headline around the path you chose in lesson one, as lesson three shows.

1. **Your path sentence,** from lesson one, added to `context/about-me.md`.
2. **A one-page CV** with a Projects section near the top: at least three of your projects from this course, each with its link, the tools named, and what it achieves.
3. **A new LinkedIn headline** that says what you do and for whom, and a Featured section with at least two project links.

Hand in a link to your CV (Google Drive or similar, anyone with the link can view) and your LinkedIn profile link. In the note, paste your path sentence and your new headline.

Before you hand in, check every line: is it true, and could you talk about it for two minutes? Anything invented, from a skill to a number, fails the assignment.
