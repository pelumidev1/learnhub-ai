Turn six weeks of work into a real next step. Choose **one** route, the one that matches your path from lesson one.

## Route A: a live offer

1. **An offer** with the four parts from lesson five: one outcome, a fixed scope with exclusions, a turnaround and a price.
2. **Live somewhere a customer can see it:** a page on your site, a Selar page, or a pinned post, with a clear way to buy or book.
3. **At least one outreach sent** to a real person who fits the offer, specific to them, with proof attached.

## Route B: three applications

1. **Three roles** that match your path.
2. **Three tailored applications sent,** each with a CV ordered for that role and a cover note that could only go to that company, as lesson four shows, each with at least one project link near the top.

## What to hand in

- **Route A:** the link to your live offer, and a screenshot of the outreach message you sent (hide the other person's details).
- **Route B:** the three job adverts' links, and your three cover notes.

In the note, say which route you chose and why, in two sentences.

## What approval looks like

- **Route A:** all four parts of the offer are present and specific; the price is stated plainly; the outreach is to a real, fitting person and is not a copy-paste blast.
- **Route B:** each application is clearly written for that company; each leads with proof; nothing on the CV or in the notes is invented.
- **Either route:** no fake testimonials, no invented numbers, no false scarcity.

You do not need a yes by the deadline. You need to have asked, properly. Then bring it to demo day.
