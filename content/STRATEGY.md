# LearnHub AI — Launch Content Strategy

_Owner: Pelumi. Voice on every platform: educational, honest, practical, story-driven. No hype, no fluff. Teach first, promote second. Never claim numbers or results we don't have — the product is pre-beta and that honesty **is** the story._

## Platforms & roles

| Platform | Role | Format | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Reach + credibility with African tech ecosystem, recruiters, ecosystem partners | 150–300 word posts, 1 image/screenshot | 2–3×/week |
| **Substack** | Depth — the journal of record for the journey | 600–1,000 word articles | 1×/week |
| **X (Twitter)** | Builders + AI community, build-in-public velocity | 8–12 tweet threads, screenshots | 2–3×/week, thread per milestone |

**Two audiences, always:** non-technical (learners, career changers, the people LearnHub serves) and technical (developers, builders, the Claude Code community). Rule of thumb: LinkedIn leans non-technical, X leans technical, Substack serves both with clearly separated sections. Pillar 3 ships explicit beginner and technical versions.

## The four pillars (files in this folder)

1. **[The product](pillar-1-product.md)** — what LearnHub AI is, who it helps, why AI makes it different. → Converts interest into beta signups.
2. **[Why I built it](pillar-2-why-i-built.md)** — the founder story: Learnhub's AWS-training origins, the financial wall, Claude Code unlocking the original vision. → Builds trust and identity.
3. **[Building it with Claude Code](pillar-3-built-with-claude-code.md)** — the full development journey, prompt by prompt. Beginner + technical versions. → The most shareable teaching asset.
4. **[Built on Fable 5 before July 12](pillar-4-fable-5.md)** — the model story: what Fable 5 changed, lessons, transition to Opus 4.8. → Timely; rides the model-launch conversation.

## Sequencing (suggested)

- **Week 1:** Pillar 2 (story first — people follow people), then Pillar 1 (what it is) two days later.
- **Week 2:** Pillar 4 (timely — publish close to the July 12 date while relevant), then Pillar 3 beginner version.
- **Week 3:** Pillar 3 technical version; open the closed beta with a build-in-public post inviting the first 10–20 users.
- **Ongoing:** one build-in-public update per real milestone (template + ready-made assets in [build-in-public.md](build-in-public.md)). Never manufacture a milestone.

## Standing rules

- Screenshots over claims. Show the actual product, actual code, actual commits.
- Say "demo mode" when the screenshot is demo mode. Say "pre-beta" until the beta opens.
- Every post ends with one concrete takeaway a reader can apply, and (where natural) one question to invite replies.
- CTA ladder: follow → subscribe to Substack → join the beta waitlist (use the deployed site link once live).
