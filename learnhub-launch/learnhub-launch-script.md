## Script A: long form, 2 min 45 sec

For YouTube, LinkedIn, and the pinned post on X. Horizontal. This is the conversion piece.

Word count is about 470. At a natural speaking pace that lands near 3:00. Trim points are marked if you need to get under 2:30.

### Cold open, 0:00 to 0:12

> Last year I shut down my own tech school.
>
> People had already paid. I had students sitting there waiting. And I still had to tell them to go somewhere else.
>
> Let me tell you what happened, and what I am doing about it now.

*Delivery: say the first line flat. No smile, no lift in energy. Then hold a full beat of silence before the second line, and let it be uncomfortable.*

### The backstory, 0:12 to 1:00

> Before all of this I was in marketing. Product marketing, digital marketing. And I spent a long time looking at tech from the outside, wanting in, with no idea where I actually fit.
>
> So I did what everybody tells you to do. I started learning AWS. Cloud. And a few months in I knew, this is not it. This is not what I want.
>
> But while I was struggling, I noticed something. A lot of people were struggling in exactly the same way. Same confusion, same dead ends.
>
> So I started Learnhub Global Academy. Our flagship was cloud engineering. I charged fifty thousand naira, about thirty dollars. Ten, fifteen people registered. One person paid a hundred and fifty thousand.
>
> And then the problems started.

*Trim point: if you need 15 seconds back, cut the "Same confusion, same dead ends" line and the "And then the problems started" line.*

### What actually went wrong, 1:00 to 1:25

> To learn cloud, every single student needed a laptop that could handle it. A working environment. A proper setup. Half of my class could not get past that, and I could not fix it for them.
>
> And I had no LMS. No platform to teach on. Building one cost money I did not have, because there was no Claude Code last year. No Codex. None of this existed.
>
> So I paused it. I found my students scholarships at another academy, and I sat with that for a long time.

*Delivery: this is the most honest stretch of the video, so slow down here. Do not rush the word "paused."*

### The turn, 1:25 to 1:55

> Here is what I kept coming back to.
>
> Nobody sat me down and taught me Gmail. Nobody sent me to school for Microsoft Word. I just started using them, and I got good.
>
> Think about our parents and mobile phones. They needed somebody to show them every single step. We did not. We just picked up the phone and used it. That is the whole difference, and it has nothing to do with being smart.
>
> People still believe that breaking into tech means becoming a coder first. Reading fifty, a hundred pages before you are allowed to build anything. That used to be true. It is not true anymore.
>
> With AI you can ship. You did not write every line, fine, somebody helped you write the code. The idea is still yours. The vision is still yours.

*Delivery: the parents-and-phones line is the best one in the script. Land it, then stop talking for a second.*

### The offer, 1:55 to 2:30

> So this is what Learnhub is now. A six week AI bootcamp for the people in the middle.
>
> Not the AI engineers building the models. Not people just chatting with ChatGPT for fun. The people who want to use AI to build something, to run a business, to finally get their work out there.
>
> Six weeks. Four things you will actually be able to do at the end.
>
> Create content with AI. Write with AI in a way that still sounds like you. Make video with AI. And build a real website or a real app with AI, with no coding.
>
> Most of it you can do from your phone. Every week you ship something. No fifty page textbook. You build it, you post it, you move.
>
> And you are not doing it alone. There is a cohort, and there is a community that keeps running after week six, because that is the part almost every course gets wrong.

### The ask, 2:30 to 2:45

> Doors open the first of September.
>
> Before that I am running a free AI masterclass next week, where I will show you the exact tools I use. Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, the connectors, how I actually build.
>
> And at that masterclass I am giving away five completely free seats into the first cohort. Show up, fill the form, that is it.
>
> I built this because I needed it and it did not exist. Come and build with me.

## Five alternate hooks

Shoot all five as separate takes at the top of the session. Four minutes of work, and it gives your editor real options. The one that wins is usually not the one you expect.

1. "Last year I shut down my own tech school. Students had already paid me." *(current pick, confession opener)*
2. "Nobody sent you to school to learn Gmail. So why do you think you need a degree to get into tech?" *(challenge opener)*
3. "I charged fifty thousand naira for a course I had to cancel. Here is what I got wrong." *(specificity opener)*
4. "Your parents needed help using a phone. You did not. Nothing about that was intelligence." *(analogy opener)*
5. "I spent months learning AWS before I realised I was learning the wrong thing entirely." *(mistake opener)*

## Shot and setup notes

**Wardrobe.** One outfit for the whole session. Change shirts and you can no longer cut between the long and short versions, or pull clips out of the long video for Reels.

**Framing.** Shoot the long version horizontal. For the short version, either reframe in post or reshoot vertical. Reshooting is better if you have the time, because reframed 16:9 crops always look reframed.

**Eyes.** Straight down the lens for the confession and for the ask. Drifting off-lens during the middle backstory is fine, it reads as thinking rather than performing.

**Do not memorise it word for word.** Learn the five beats: I shut it down, here is why, here is what I realised, here is what it is now, here is how to get in. Then say them in your own words. Treat the script as a target to aim at rather than lines to recite. A slightly imperfect sentence in your real voice beats a perfect one that sounds read.

**Silence between beats.** Leave two full seconds of silence between sections. Your editor gets clean cut points, and it stops you rushing.

## After the shoot

Once you have the raw file, two skills you already have installed handle the rest:

```bash
claude
```

Then inside the session:

- `/transcribe` on the video file to get a clean transcript
- `/youtube-description` on the same file to generate the title, description, timestamps and tags in one pass

Run the captions and post copy through `/humanizer` before publishing. Copy that reads like a template undoes the work the video just did.
