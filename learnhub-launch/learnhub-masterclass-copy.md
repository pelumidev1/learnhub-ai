## 4. The giveaway form

Opens during the masterclass, closes when the session ends. Say the URL out loud twice and drop it in the chat three times.

### Intro text on the form

> Five free seats in the six week Learnhub bootcamp, starting September.
>
> Three questions. Two minutes. Be specific rather than impressive, because specific is what I am actually reading for.
>
> Closing when this session ends. Winners announced Friday 28 August.

### Identity fields

| Field | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full name | Text | Yes |
| Email | Email | Yes, and use the one you registered with |
| WhatsApp number | Phone | Yes |

WhatsApp is required here, unlike on registration. Winners need contacting fast and the cohort community runs on WhatsApp anyway.

### The three questions

**Question 1**

> What do you want to build or sell with AI in the next six weeks?
>
> *Be specific. "A website for my catering business so people can see the menu and message me" tells me more than "I want to learn AI."*

Long text, required.

**Question 2**

> Why now?
>
> *What changed, or what have you been putting off?*

Long text, required.

**Question 3**

> The bootcamp is six weeks. One live call a week, and one project due every week. Can you commit to that?
>
> - Yes, I can commit to both
> - Yes, but I would need to know the call time first
> - No, not for the next six weeks

Single choice, required.

That third question does real work. It sets the expectation before anyone accepts a seat, and anyone answering "no" has saved you a dropout.

### After they submit

> Got it. Winners announced Friday 28 August by email and WhatsApp.
>
> If you do not get a seat, I will send you something worth having instead. Watch your inbox on Friday.

## 6. Notes for whoever builds the page

- The giveaway form URL must not be live before the session starts. If it leaks early the attendance filter stops working, which is the only thing making this giveaway worth running
- Registration emails feed one list. That list is the launch asset, not the video
- Tag people who filled the optional "what do you want to build" field on registration. They are your warmest leads and deserve a personal message, not just the sequence
- Do not put a price anywhere on the masterclass registration page. The masterclass is free and the bootcamp price belongs on the bootcamp page, which is a different page going live 1 September
- Headings stay sentence case. No em dashes anywhere
