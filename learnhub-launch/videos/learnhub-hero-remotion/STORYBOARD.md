# LearnHub AI Bootcamp hero film, v2 storyboard

Built in Remotion. 1920×1080, 30 fps, 45s. Muted-first; ElevenLabs voice later.
Shot-for-shot on the Manus 2.0 reference (`../learnhub-bootcamp-hero/reference/REFERENCE.md` §9 and §10).
We copy its structure, timing, transitions and type treatment. We do not copy its logo, wording or photos.

## Rules taken from the reference

- **Every seam is a hard cut**, except one cursor-click circle reveal (week 3).
- **Titles are small and quiet.** General Sans 500, about 44 to 60px, sentence case, ink on paper `#F6F7FB`. One word per line goes royal blue `#1F33CC`. Only "Six" in the opener goes huge (about 300px), once.
- **Serif only inside the product UI** (Instrument Serif), as the reference does.
- **Words arrive by blur, not slide.** Each word goes from 16px blur to sharp in 4 to 5 frames. The next starts 2 to 3 frames later. A later word sometimes drops in from slightly raised.
- **Held shots push in** about 3%.
- **The word wheel is the spine.** It appears before every chapter: "Build" fixed in blue, with the list rolling one step on a 3D drum. The centre item is white; neighbours are smaller and grey.
- **The app fills the frame**, with no browser chrome and no caption pill. The camera moves inside it.
- **Payoffs sit on full-bleed photos** with Apple glass on top. Landscapes go behind words; students go behind results.

## Photos needed (4, generated)

1. Lagos lagoon at dawn: calm, pale blue and warm white, wide, no people up close (shot 6).
2. A calm highland or coastal landscape in East or West Africa, soft haze, room in the centre for a glass card (shot 9).
3. A young Nigerian woman at a laptop by a window, warm light, shallow depth of field, room on one side (shots 20 and 21).
4. A young African man presenting at a small demo day, a screen behind him, shallow depth of field (shot 25).

All four in one style: natural light, soft contrast, pale blues and warm whites, editorial, not stock-photo smiles.

## Sound

Scratch mix: a short whoosh on each wheel roll, clicks on cursor presses, soft pops on ticks and chips, and a bass hit on the end card. No chimes, no sparkles.
