# Brand images

The logo files in this folder are described in `CLAUDE.md` (Brand). Leave them
where they are and keep their names: `logoo.JPG` and the two UUID-named JPEGs
are the source artwork, the `Learnhub … background.PNG` files are the exported
marks, and the three `logo-mark*.svg` files are what the app uses.

## No photograph slots right now

The site uses no photographs since the 2026-10-02 redesign: the landing,
sign-in and app screens are drawn in code (the lesson mockup, the roadmap
mockups, the floating course cards). The photos and clips the old landing used
(`students-hero.webp`, the `step-*` and `after-*` cards, `signup.png`, the
`public/media/` clips and the one `paths/` object) were deleted the same day.
They are in git history if one is ever wanted back.

## If you add one

- **Keep files light.** Under ~300 KB each. The audience is on metered mobile
  data, so a heavy photo costs them real money. Export as JPG at ~75–80%
  quality, or WebP.
- **Wide photos for wide spaces.** Image slots usually cover-crop from the
  centre, so the shape you supply matters more than the exact pixel size.
- **People, not stock clichés.** Real African students and young professionals
  working on phones and laptops.
